Proximal gradient method with the classical Armijo backtracking: each search starts from the last accepted stepsize
(no increase) and halves it until the descent condition holds, PG-LS(1, 0.5).
Reported: iterations to reach residual <= 1e-5, counting only the iterations before the stepsize falls below 1e-8. Near the
solution the descent test can fail because of rounding errors, and since the stepsize cannot increase it then collapses;
the residuals after that point are not meaningful. On Dual-max-entropy the stepsize is reduced to ~1e-4 in the first
iterations (large curvature at the starting point) and cannot increase afterwards; the residual stays above 1.
Same instances, starting points, initial stepsize procedure and tolerance as in the paper. Residual: for FISTA the
gradient mapping at its returned point x_k; for NPG2 and PG-LS the residual ||x_{k+1} - x_k|| / t_k of the paper.
NPG2: from the main result files results/<problem>/ (available from the link in README.md).
